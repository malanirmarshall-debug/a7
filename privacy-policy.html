Privacy Policy

Understand how we collect, store, and process your client registration data.

Client Data Collection

When you register an account or request bespoke pattern fitting in our New York observation showroom,
we collect private data including your physical address, phone contact, email, and wax monogram lettering choices.
This information is used strictly to authenticate purchase records and maintain custom pressing portfolios.
Veltranova never distributes, leases, or trades client details to external textile distribution networks,
safeguarding your personal information under traditional bank-grade digital encryption algorithms and protocols.

Encryption & Fabric Registries

Your watch ownership and physical serial keys are stored inside our secure private database systems.
This ensures that if your gown order is delayed, our customer service centers can match registry records.
We implement multiple validation keys before authorizing database registry modifications or handoff updates,
preventing unauthorized registrations of luxury silks and maintaining authentic garment provenance records.
Security certifications are inspected quarterly by lead cybersecurity firms to guarantee absolute protection.

Showroom Security Measures

Our New York showroom uses physical, structural, and electronic safety gear to monitor raw materials.
Only certified tailors have clearance to enter secure assembly vaults where organic cotton and wool are stored.
Furthermore, velvet tray orders submitted for restoration are tracked under custom multi-key identification protocols,
ensuring your heirloom piece is cataloged, secure, and monitored at every stage of the fabric-dyeing process.
Any security incident triggers automated alerts to regional emergency departments and local police networks.

Client Right to Registry Erasure

Registered Veltranova owners retain the right to erase their personal digital details from our systems.
To clear your physical address or phone contact history, contact our Mercer registry concierge team directly.
Please note that erasing owner verification keys can complicate subsequent warranty claims and mending updates,
as our weavers require valid order history before authorizing lifetime mechanical servicing and thread replacements.
Erasure procedures are finalized within ten business days after official phone or email authentication.

Policy Modifications and Disclosures

Veltranova reserves the right to modify this client data protocol to match global luxury retail directives.
We notify registered lounge owners via email whenever major structural changes occur in our database systems.
Your continued engagement with our New York showroom indicates acceptance of our updated privacy terms,
which remain accessible at our concierge portal and footer link arrays for transparent reference review.
For further queries regarding database storage, contact our New York safety officer via email concierge.
