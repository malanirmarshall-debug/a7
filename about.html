A Commitment to Velvet

Veltranova was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of manual velvet weaving and introduce it to luxury home organizers. In a world increasingly saturated by quick synthetic threads and mass-processed glued seams, we dedicate our workshop to hand-welted fabric frames and custom patterns. We believe a velvet lining is not simply a fabric cut; it is a representation of drawer comfort, weight distribution, and master loom weaving.

Each organic velvet thread and lining material is clicked individually inside sustainable farming mills in Alsace, France. Once received at our New York showroom, the organizer frames are hand-clicked, hand-basted, and hot-pressed entirely by hand. Organic protective waxes use natural beeswaxes and mineral lubricants instead of cheap synthetic glazes, protecting lining softness and leg elasticities. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our master weavers to sustain a Lifetime Quality Pledge.

Our Three Workshop Pillars

01. Fiber Sourcing

We draw stitch paths manually under strict templates, forming tailored outlines.

02. Pile Clicking

Stitching pocket walls, packing interlining, and welting drawer bands slowly.

03. Organic Linens

Applying silk linings, testing velvet tension, and hot pressing gold initials.

The Weaving Future

As Veltranova looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-weave principles. We maintain a small workshop of certified weavers, limiting our global output to a few hundred velvet sets per year. This exclusivity ensures that every client registered receives the undivided focus and care of our loom masters. Veltranova is a lifetime commitment to velvet clicks, pile calibrations, and heritage weaving loom preservation.
