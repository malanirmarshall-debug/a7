Cookie Policy

Understand how we use cookies and tracking systems on our boutique storefront.

Understanding Digital Cookies

Veltranova utilizes digital cookies to optimize user interactions on our online cozy apparel portal.
Cookies are simple, secure text files placed on your local browser by our web servers during visits.
These systems allow our platform to remember your page preferences, sizing filters, and material selections,
creating a streamlined editorial experience that highlights relevant organic cotton and wool gown lines.
Cookies do not scan local computer hard drives or execute programs that could compromise system safety.

Essential Boutique Cookies

Certain cookies are required to allow basic checkout actions and secure showroom consultation scheduling.
These files track session keys and authentication status to prevent data loss during catalog navigations.
Our web servers disable these cookies immediately when you close the active browser window or tab session,
leaving no residual files on your machine that could track your external web activity or search history.
Blocking essential cookies through browser options will disable our custom reservation and inquiry forms.

Analytical Performance Tracking

We employ analytical cookies to measure visitor counts, popular watch categories, and load latency.
These metrics help our design team fix navigation issues, optimize image sizes, and improve overall speeds.
We aggregate cookie metrics anonymously, ensuring individual watch collectors are not targeted or cataloged,
in absolute compliance with modern digital user privacy laws and European global tracking standards.
You can opt out of analytical tracking without losing access to our horological articles and galleries.

Bespoke Advertising Settings

Veltranova partners with luxury advertising networks to display tailored velvet robes on external platforms.
These promotional cookies monitor which lounge models you view on our primary index pages,
allowing partner platforms to show customized boutique advertisements that match your visual design taste.
These networks do not collect names, addresses, phone hotlines, or custom engraving registry codes,
maintaining strict separations between private client identity and general web browsing interest indexes.

Managing Cookie Permissions

You can configure your browser to block cookies or notify you before a cookie file is saved.
Browser configuration panels let you delete stored cookies periodically to reset tracking history indexes.
Adjusting these permissions will not restrict access to our watch images and brand history details,
although interactive tools like the product tab switcher or reservation calendar may load slower.
For guidance on resetting permissions, review your browser help directory files or contact support.
